## H-0 approval request

Local preparation is reviewable ([evidence](services/api/results/h0-local-images-20261001-01.json), [drafts](deploy/aws/README.md)); nothing exists in AWS. Please decide:

1. **Bounded private test:** at most two simultaneous tasks, at most two hours, $10 pre-tax ceiling (estimate about $0.40 compute + ALB, plus ECR/logs/transfer for about 1.1 GB of compressed images), and the [cleanup procedure](deploy/aws/README.md#cleanup-procedure-for-a-future-bounded-h-0-test). Also say whether evidence images may stay in ECR afterwards (about 1.1 GB, about $0.11/month). A billing alert is not a hard cap; the test stops on schedule.
2. **Region and ingress:** proposed `il-central-1`; ALB + ACM HTTPS on 443 restricted to the reviewer range; task in a public subnet with a public IPv4, no NAT.
3. **First configuration:** proposed 1 vCPU / 3 GiB with Photon (held locally: no OOM, MOTIS 64%, Photon 53%); fallback 0.5 vCPU / 2 GiB without addresses (unmeasured; its 128 MiB verifier peaked at 120 MiB here, and startup would roughly double).
4. **8 GiB ceiling basis:** allocated (two 3 GiB tasks = 6 GiB, fits) or measured (about 3.4 GiB for two tasks)? It matters only if 4 GiB tasks become necessary.
5. **Access and names:** the AWS access method (for example a dedicated IAM Identity Center user or scoped profile with MFA and a billing alert; credentials never enter Git or chat), the reviewer IP range (CIDR), and a DNS name you control for ACM validation.
