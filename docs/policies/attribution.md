# Attribution and data sources

**DRAFT — not legal advice, pending review**

Prepared 1 October 2026. This page is proposed publication text; it does not clear
unresolved source terms or replace notices required in distributed artifacts.

**© OpenStreetMap contributors.** OpenStreetMap data is available under the
[Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
See [OpenStreetMap copyright and licence information](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
Routing/geocoding use OSM extracts supplied by [Geofabrik](https://www.geofabrik.de/data/download.html).
Show this attribution visibly wherever OSM-based results/maps are presented and in
API metadata/documentation before release. Public use of derived databases may carry
share-alike/access obligations; graphs, reference databases and Photon indexes require
an ODbL review before public distribution. Tile services have separate usage policies.

**Public transport schedules: Israel Ministry of Transport and Road Safety (MOT),
GTFS and TripIdToDate.** The accepted inputs are the 60-day GTFS product and its dated
trip mapping. Their precise licence, required attribution wording and redistribution
permissions remain unresolved in the [data-access record](../data-usage-and-access.md).
This credit is provisional and does not imply MOT endorsement or accepted terms.

MOTIS routes journeys (MIT); Photon provides optional address geocoding (Apache-2.0).
Their software licences do not license the datasets they process. See the
[licence audit](../licence-audit.md) for dependency and container notice obligations.
The OpenTransit code licence remains a user decision. No live SIRI or alerts attribution
is claimed because those capabilities are deferred.
