## English (reference translation)

**To:** ptsupport@mot.gov.il
**Subject:** Follow-up: request of 21.9.2026 — GTFS data terms of use — OpenTransit Israel

Hello,

On 21 September 2026 I wrote to you about access to public transport data, and its terms of use, for OpenTransit Israel, an open-source public transport trip planner. I have not yet received a reply. Could you confirm that the request was received, and give me its reference number if one was assigned?

For now the project uses the public GTFS files (israel-public-transportation.zip and TripIdToDate.zip). Before running a public service, I would be grateful for a written answer to these questions:

1. **Terms of use:** are the terms on the gov.il "Static information - GTFS" page the complete terms for the GTFS and TripIdToDate files? Do the data.gov.il licence or any other licence also apply?
2. **Display and redistribution:** may we show the public processed results based on the data (journeys, departure times and stops) on a website, in an app and through a public API? May we redistribute the files themselves, or parts of them, such as small test samples in the open-source repository?
3. **Attribution:** is specific attribution wording required? If so, what is it?
4. **Approved uses:** are there limits on keeping earlier versions of the files for testing and reproducibility, or on any other uses we should know about?
5. **Statistics derived from Hasadna's archive:** the Public Knowledge Workshop (Hasadna) publishes a public archive of the Ministry's realtime (SIRI) data. May we show the public aggregate statistics computed from that data, such as delay distributions and line reliability figures? If so, with what attribution? (We will also ask Hasadna separately.)

The questions in my earlier email about realtime (SIRI) and service-alert access still stand, but they are not urgent at this stage.
